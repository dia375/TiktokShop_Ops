A thought worth exploring

Food for thought · From first post to first sale

Grow creators in stages, with a campaign built for each one.

A creator with zero sales needs something very different from one already selling every week. Grouping creators by stage could make it easier to give each one the right push at the right time.

Stage 1 · New

No sales yet

Just joined. Needs confidence and a simple first step.

Campaign idea: first-sale challenge with easy products to start with

Stage 2 · Rising

Selling, but not steady

Some sales. Needs consistency and better content.

Campaign idea: weekly posting goal with content tips from top creators

Stage 3 · Established

Selling every week

Strong results. Needs scale and bigger brand deals.

Campaign idea: early access to brand campaigns and LIVE events

Could start

Sort creators by stage

  • Pick a few creators in a new market.
  • Group them by sales, posting and activity.
  • Run one campaign per stage.

Could learn

Let the data decide

  • Track activity and sales each week.
  • See who moves up a stage and why.
  • Change what isn't working quickly.

Could grow

A simple playbook

  • Turn what worked into a stage-by-stage guide.
  • Share it with cross-functional teams.
  • Reuse it for each new market launch.
